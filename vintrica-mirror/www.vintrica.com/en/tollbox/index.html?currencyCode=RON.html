How can I use the toll box in Europe?

Reserved lanes in 5 countries

Benefit from reserved lanes in all supported countries: Liber-t (France), Telepass (Italy), VIA-T (Spain), Via Verde (Portugal) and ENC (Croatia).

Instructions: How to drive through the toll booth

  • Approach the barrier slowly.
  • Stop briefly until you hear a beep indicating recognition of your toll box.
  • The barrier opens automatically.
  • Just drive on and enjoy your journey without waiting.
  • The toll fees are conveniently billed via your customer account.
  • Always pay attention to the local traffic lights and instructions.

Frequently asked questions

How does the toll box work?

The toll box is attached to the windscreen. The barrier at toll booths opens automatically and the charge is billed directly via your stored payment method.

Where do I attach the toll box in the car?
Attach the toll box to the inside of your windscreen next to the rear-view mirror. Doing so optimises detection at toll booths.
Note: If the vehicles has an athermal windscreen (thermal insulation glazing), the box must be fitted in the specially designated, uncoated area (usually a dotted area around the rear-view mirror).
Can I also use the toll box for my motorbike?

Of course, the toll box can also be used on a motorbike. We recommend storing it in a tank bag that protects it from rain and cold. Make sure that the box is positioned in the rucksack facing forwards – so in the direction of travel.

Is this one box really valid for all 5 countries?
Yes, absolutely. This one toll box is accepted by toll systems in France, Italy, Spain, Portugal and most motorways in Croatia. You do not need separate devices or vignettes for toll systems on these routes.
What does “use" mean with vintrica Flow?

Use is defined as any time an electronic system run by a toll system or car park operator records the reads the vintrica Flow box (e.g. when entering a motorway, moving between two motorways, exiting the motorway and entering or exiting a car park, etc.).
