vintrica Flow – Temporary Service Disruption

Due to a technical issue with the highway operator’s IT infrastructure, vintrica Flow toll boxes are currently not being detected at toll stations. As a result, toll barriers may not open automatically.

The highway operator is working urgently to resolve the issue.

Until the issue has been resolved, please do not use dedicated toll box lanes. Please use regular toll lanes and pay the toll directly at the toll station.

We will update this notice as soon as the service is fully operational again.
