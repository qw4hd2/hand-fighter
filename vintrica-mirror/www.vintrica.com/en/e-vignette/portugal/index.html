Vignette Portugal

Tolls in Portugal are largely managed by private companies, which allocate the revenue to maintaining and expanding the infrastructure. Nearly all motorways in Portugal are subject to tolls, while the rest of the road network is toll-free. Traditional adhesive toll stickers are not used in Portugal.

Toll costs vary depending on the vehicle class, route, and chosen payment method. There are no standardised rates, as each operator sets their own prices.

Motorways subject to tolls

Although nearly all motorways in Portugal are subject to tolls, it is important to pay attention to the type of motorway. The private motorway operators are responsible for the motorway regulations. Toll motorways in Portugal are divided into conventional motorways and motorways with electronic tolls.

Exceptions

Starting from 1 January 2025, the following motorways will no longer be subject to tolls:

  • A4 (sections)
  • A13
  • A13-1
  • A22
  • A23
  • A24
  • A25
  • A28 (sections)

Conventional motorways

On conventional motorways, tolls are charged at toll stations. The distance from station to station is calculated. If it takes longer than twelve hours to reach the desired station, an additional fee is charged. This can be twice as high as the initial charge. Payment is made in cash or by credit card.

Motorways with electronic tolls

Electronic tolls are not available for all toll motorways in Portugal. If you want to drive on a motorway with an electronic toll, you can choose between several options to validate the toll for your vehicle.

  • vintrica Flow Box: This toll device is compatible with the Via Verde lanes. It is installed in the vehicle and enables automatic toll payment via the registered payment method. In addition to Portugal, it can also be used in other Southern European countries such as Italy and France. The device can be ordered online and conveniently delivered to your home.
  • Via Verde Visitors: With the Via Verde Transponder, it is not only possible to pass all electronically tolled routes, but also to drive on conventional routes with the Via Verde Lane. The toll is charged to the credit card account on file after passing the toll station. You can order and pay for the transmitter online.
  • Easy Toll: This system allows you to register your licence plate for a period of up to one month. When you register, you link your credit card to your account. When you drive through a toll barrier, your licence plate is photographed, and the toll is debited from your credit card.
  • Toll Card: This system allows you to assign a prepaid card to your licence plate. This is available in denominations of € 5, € 10, € 20, and € 40 (£ 4.31, £ 8.61, £ 17.23, and £ 34.45). When you pass through an electronic toll barrier, your licence plate number is photographed, and the toll charge is debited from the TOLLCard.
  • Virtual Prepaid-Card (formerly TollService): The virtual prepaid card is designed for short stays in Portugal. The toll is paid in advance and is determined by the vehicle class. For vehicle classes 1 and 2, you can choose between a 3-day prepaid card or a route-defined prepaid card. The route-defined prepaid card is only valid for one route, while the 3-day prepaid card is valid for all routes.

The electronic route sections can be identified by the blue sign.

Regulations for cars, motorcycle, caravans and campervans

Cars Depending on the height at the front axle (up to/from 1.1 m)
Trailers Trailers and semi-trailers with a towing vehicle up to 1.1 metres high at the front axle are classified as passenger cars
Trailers and semi-trailers with a towing vehicle over 1.1 metres high at the front axle and depending on the number of axles of the overall trailer and semi-trailer combination
Campervans Depending on the number of axles

Vehicle categories

Category 1 Motorcycles and cars (including those with trailers) with a height of less than 1.1 metres at the front axle
Category 2 Motor vehicles with two axles and a height of 1.1 metres or higher at the front axle
Category 3 Motor vehicles with three axles and a height of 1.1 metres or higher at the front axle
Category 4 Motor vehicles with more than three axles and a height of 1.1 metres or higher at the front axle

Fines and penalties

If you disregard the toll regulations in Portugal with a foreign vehicle, you could face a fine of between £ 21.53 and £ 107.66. You will also be asked to pay an administrative fee.
If you fail to pay the toll with a vehicle registered in Portugal, for example a hire car, you can pay the toll retrospectively at any post office after two days. If you are unable to pay the toll within five days, you will also be fined. It becomes very expensive if the car hire company takes care of the reimbursement of the toll and pays it.
