E-vignette Hungary

Tolls in Hungary are collected through a digital vignette. Motorway vignettes are linked to a vehicle registration number and can be bought online at vintrica. You can purchase the electronic vignette quickly and easily using PayPal, online banking or a credit card. A confirmation will be sent to your e-mail address and, if you wish, you will receive a text message confirmation that you can show at a checkpoint.

Toll motorways

In Hungary, all toll motorways and expressways are marked with an "M" (e-Matrica). Valid e-vignettes are required on the following Hungarian motorways:

  • The M0 motorway is the central element of the motorway network. You need a motorway vignette for the following sections:
    • Between the M3 and the main road 2
    • Between the M5 and the main road 4
  • M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, M6, M7, M15, M19, M25, M30, M35, M43, M60, M70, M85 and M86.

From 8 June 2025, the following routes will also be subject to tolls:

M44 Expressway:

  • Kecskemét North – Szentkirály (Pest County)
    → Bács-Kiskun county vignette is sufficient; Pest county vignette is only required if no valid Bács-Kiskun vignette is held.

  • Kecskemét North – Tiszaug/Tiszakürt (Bács-Kiskun County)
    → Bács-Kiskun county vignette required.

  • Lakitelek/Tiszakécske – Szarvas/Szentes (Jász-Nagykun-Szolnok County)
    → Jász-Nagykun-Szolnok county vignette required.

  • Szolnok/Martfű/Kunszentmárton – Békéscsaba-Gyula/Debrecen-Szeged (Békés County)
    → Békés county vignette required.

M85 Motorway:

  • Győr West – Sopron – National border (Győr-Moson-Sopron County)
    → Győr-Moson-Sopron county vignette required.

Important information: National e-vignettes (e.g. daily, weekly or annual vignettes) remain valid on the newly tolled sections.

Exceptions

Some toll-free motorways and expressways are an exception and do not require a vignette:

  • M8
  • M31
  • Pécs Southwest bypass from M60 motorway between roads 58 and 5826
  • M9 between main road 6 and main road 51
  • M9 (sign: main road 61) Kaposvár bypass
  • M4 (sign: main road 4) between Vecsés and the road to Budapest Liszt Ferenc Airport (between 19+550 km and 20+518 km)
  • The following sections of the M0 motorway:
    • Between main road 1 and motorway M5
    • Between motorway M4 (main road 4) and motorway M3
    • Megyeri Bridge (between main road 11 and main road 2)

County vignettes

In Hungary, you have the option of purchasing annual vignettes for individual counties (= federal states). The so-called county vignettes are valid for a complete vignette year. The current annual vignette is valid from 1 January 2026 to 31 January 2027.

You can buy a single e-vignette for the following counties: Bács-Kiskun, Baranya, Békés, Borsod-Abaúj-Zemplén, Budapest, Fejér, Csongrád-Csanád, Győr-Moson-Sopron, Hajdú-Bihar, Heves, Jász-Nagykum-Szolnok, Komárom-Esztergom, Nógrád, Pest, Somogy, Szabolcs-Szatmár-Bereg, Tolna, Vas, Veszprém, Zala.

Toll regulations for lorries

In Hungary, instead of paying a flat-rate toll for a lorry, you have to pay a route-dependent toll. This is based on the kilometres driven, the permissible total weight, the number of axles, the road type and the environmental class. You must register the lorry toll in the Hungarian HU-GO system.

Fines

If you drive without a valid e-vignette, you risk heavy fines. We therefore recommend buying a vignette for your category before you set off.

How long are you allowed to drive in Hungary without a vignette?

If you accidentally drive on a route requiring a vignette and notice this, you still have 60 minutes to buy a valid vignette. You will then not receive a fine.

How is the vignette obligation checked?

Compliance with the car toll on motorways is monitored using video surveillance. The cameras are often located directly after the border crossing.

What penalties do I face without an e-vignette?

You will receive a fine by post. This has to be paid within 60 days, otherwise the fine will increase considerably.

Can I transfer my Hungarian vignette?

Yes, you can transfer the e-vignette to other vehicle registration numbers. However, this is only possible if the vehicle owner changes. In case of a write-off or theft, the e-vignette can be transferred to another vehicle of the same toll class.
For more information, contact the toll operator's customer service department:

Tel: +36(36)587-500
E-mail: ugyfel@nemzetiutdij.hu

Can I change my number plate at a later date?

Before the start of validity: You can change the licence plate of the vignette before the first day of validity if you use the flex service offered by vintrica.com.

During validity: In the event of an incorrectly entered registration number or country code with a maximum deviation of 3 characters, you can submit the correct registration number on site at a customer service office of the National Toll Payment Services PLC within 60 days from the start of the validity of the vignette. For the procedure you need the registration certificate, confirmation of the purchase of the vignette and your passport. The fee for changing the vignette is HUF 1470 (approx. £3.45). If you have mixed up the 0 (zero, the number) and the O (o, the letter), the change is free of charge.

Can I postpone the start of validity after purchase?

Only the flex service at vintrica.com offers you the option of changing the validity period at any time before the first day of validity.

Which vehicles are exempt from the vignette?

Only the following organisations are exempt:

  • The Hungarian Armed Forces for the protection of public order and NATO
  • bodies for the management of the public road network
  • Civil protection
  • Transport authorities
  • Bodies for toll collection and monitoring tasks
  • Other bodies with authorisation to use
