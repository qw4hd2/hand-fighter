Tolls in Bosnia and Herzegovina

The toll regulations in Bosnia and Herzegovina may seem confusing and unclear at first glance. There are different rules and charges that can vary depending on the type of road, vehicle category and distance travelled. In Bosnia and Herzegovina, tolls are charged on a distance-based system on certain motorway sections. To help you avoid unpleasant surprises and delays, we provide all the essential information you need to understand the applicable toll regulations.

Toll motorways

Not all motorways in Bosnia and Herzegovina are subject to tolls. Charges apply on certain sections of the A1 motorway as well as on two other motorways:

  • A1 near Sarajevo: Zenica – Tarčin
  • A1 near Mostar: Međugorje – Ljubuški
  • E661: Banja Luka – Gradiška
  • Autoput: Banja Luka – Doboj

Regulations for Cars, motorcycles, trailers and campervans

Regulations for Cars

Cars are weighted differently depending on the height of the front axle (up to 1.3m/over 1.3m).

Regulations for Trailers

Trailer combinations are tolled differently depending on the height of the front axle of the towing vehicle, the number of axles and, in some cases, the weight.

Regulations for campervans

Campervans are subject to different tolls depending on the height of the front axle, the number of axles and, in some cases, the weight.

Payment

Payment at toll stations

The toll is usually paid in cash at the toll stations. You can pay in the Bosnian currency Convertible Mark (BAM) or in Euros. Almost all toll stations offer payment by debit and credit card.

Electronic payment

In the territory of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, it is possible to pay tolls on the A1 motorway electronically using the ACC transponder in the pre-payment procedure. The transponder system offers several advantages. On the one hand, users receive an attractive discount of around 20% on the toll charges. On the other hand, the transponder's credit can be recharged and is valid for an unlimited period of time. The transponder can be purchased at any toll station and is linked to the vehicle licence plate number.

In the sub-regions of the Republika Srpska there is a separate regulation for tolls on the motorways around Banja Luka. Here it is possible to pay the toll electronically using the ENP transmitter. This device can be purchased at the toll stations in Jakupovci, Prnjavor and Kladari. It offers drivers a convenient and time-saving way to pay the toll.

Prices and terms

In Bosnia and Herzegovina, tolls are charged on certain motorway sections based on the distance travelled. The price depends on the entry and exit points at the toll stations. Fees

Anyone who does not pay the toll in Bosnia and Herzegovina must expect a fine of at least 87 pounds. Compliance with the toll is strictly monitored in Bosnia and Herzegovina. If you do not pay the fine, the amount of the fine can increase even further.

Can the toll/vignette be refunded?

Toll refunds are not possible in Bosnia and Herzegovina. A refund is usually only possible in countries that offer an electronic vignette.
