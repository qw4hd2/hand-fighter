Campaign explained

What is the VMC shirt campaign?

The VMC shirt campaign combines Europe-wide roadside assistance from the vintrica Mobility Club with real football excitement. When you take out a VMC annual membership, you receive a limited-edition shirt free of charge.

The campaign runs until 31/07/2026 and is only available while stocks last.
