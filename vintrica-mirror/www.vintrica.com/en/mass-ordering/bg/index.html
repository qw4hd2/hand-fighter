country

Two-letter abbreviation of the country of registration, e.g. CH

The country code, also known as the Alpha-2 code, is a two-letter abbreviation that identifies the country of origin of a vehicle. This code is defined for each country according to the ISO 3166-1 standard.

Sometimes the country code differs from the abbreviation on the license plate. In such cases, the country in which the vehicle is officially registered is decisive. Examples: Germany = DE, France = FR
