About Hand Fighter

Hand Fighter started with a simple question: what if you could play a fighting game without touching anything? Instead of mashing buttons, you show your hand to the camera — an open palm to move, a fist to punch, two fingers to kick, three fingers to block, and a raised hand to jump.

How it works

The game uses on-device hand-tracking technology that runs inside your browser. It looks at the shape of your hand about thirty times per second and turns it into fighting-game inputs. Because everything runs locally, it works without an account, without a download, and without ever sending your camera video anywhere.

What's in the game

  • Six fighters — Blaze, Frost, Volt, Onyx, Kira and Sensei — each with different speed, power and health
  • Three stages — a neon night city, a lantern-lit dojo and a sunset beach
  • Arcade ladder — beat all six opponents in a row with rising difficulty
  • Local 2-player — two friends, one camera, one hand each
  • Online matches — create a room, share a 4-letter code, fight a friend anywhere in the world
  • Combos, blocking, hit-freeze, damage-trail health bars and a cinematic camera that follows the action

Who made it

Hand Fighter is an independent project built by a small team who love fighting games and wanted to try something new with camera technology. It's free to play and supported by ads. If you enjoy it, the best way to support us is to share it with a friend — and then beat them online.
