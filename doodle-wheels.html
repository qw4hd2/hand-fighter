How it works

Watch the track

The camera looks ahead of the car. Stairs, mud, steel spikes and water pools each have a clear look, and each one beats a different wheel.

Draw a wheel

Sketch any closed shape on the pad, or tap a preset: circle, square, star or paddle. The game slows down while you draw so you always have time.

Swap in time

The new wheels appear on the car instantly and it keeps rolling. Time it right and the obstacle becomes a speed bump. Get it wrong and you learn fast.

What's in the game

🎨Draw anything

Your drawing becomes a real physics object: its size, points and balance all change how the car behaves.

🏁Endless levels

Tracks are generated as you go, adding new obstacles every few levels. Earn up to three stars per level for fast, clean runs.

🎯Lean to save it

Hold left or right to lean the car back or forward mid-air. Checkpoints are frequent, so mistakes cost seconds, not levels.

🔊Feels alive

Engine hum that follows your speed, dust on dirt, splashes in water, and a camera shake when you land hard.

Frequently asked questions

Is Doodle Wheels free?

Yes, completely. It runs in your browser with nothing to install. The site is supported by ads.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Hold the phone sideways, draw on the pad in the corner with your finger, and touch the left or right half of the screen to lean.

My drawing didn't do anything.

The shape was probably too small. Draw one closed loop that fills a good part of the pad. Anything that pokes out of the loop acts like a spike or a paddle blade.

The car got stuck or flipped. Did I lose?

No. If the car stays flipped or stops moving for a few seconds, it restarts from the last checkpoint, which is never far away. A wrong wheel is a second lost, not a level lost.

Do I need a camera like in Hand Fighter?

No camera at all. Doodle Wheels is played with a finger or a mouse only.
