Controls

How to play

Stand in front of your camera, give yourself a little room, and use these gestures:

✋Open palmlean left / right to move
☝️Raise handlift high to jump
✊Fistpunch
✌️Two fingerskick
🤟Three fingersblock

No camera? Use the keyboard (A/D move · W jump · S block · F punch · G kick) or the on-screen touch buttons on your phone. Full setup tips in the hand tracking guide.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Hand Fighter really free?

Yes. It runs in your browser with nothing to install. The site is supported by ads.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — it works in Chrome or Safari on Android and iPhone. Hold your phone sideways for the best view. If hand tracking runs slow on an older phone, the on-screen touch buttons work great.

Is my camera video uploaded anywhere?

No. Hand tracking runs entirely on your own device. No video is ever sent, stored or shared. You can also turn hand tracking off and play with keyboard or touch.

How does online play work?

One player clicks Create Room and gets a 4-letter code. The other types the code and joins. The match runs live between your two browsers — each of you can use your own camera or keyboard.

Which gestures are the most reliable?

Keep your hand inside the camera view with your palm facing the camera, in decent light. A clear fist and a clear open palm are the two gestures you'll use most.
