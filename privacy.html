Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Your camera

Hand Fighter can use your device's camera to recognize hand gestures. All hand tracking happens entirely on your own device, inside your browser. No video, images or camera data are ever uploaded, transmitted, stored or shared with us or anyone else. The camera is only activated when you start a match, only after your browser asks for your permission, and you can turn hand tracking off at any time and play with the keyboard or touch controls instead.

Online multiplayer

When you play online, your browser connects directly to your opponent's browser (peer-to-peer). Like any internet connection, this exposes your IP address to the other player and to the connection/relay servers that establish the link. Only game inputs and game state (positions, health, moves) are exchanged — never audio or video. No accounts are required and no personal information is collected.

Data stored on your device

The game saves a few preferences in your browser's local storage (for example your chosen difficulty, whether sound is muted, and whether hand tracking is enabled). This data never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Advertising and cookies

This website may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and other third-party ad networks. These vendors may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or www.aboutads.info.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting, aggregated analytics to understand how many people visit the site and which pages are popular. This information is not used to identify individual visitors.

Children

Hand Fighter is a cartoon fighting game suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
